## Add your app

Open a PR adding one row to the community table:

1. **Shipped or public-TestFlight apps only** — a link someone can actually tap (App Store, TestFlight public link, or landing page).
2. One-liner = your app's mission sentence. If the wizard wrote your `DECISIONS/000-mission.md`, you already have it.
3. Keep the row honest and the link safe-for-everyone. Maintainers may decline entries that conflict with [docs/NOT_FOR.md](docs/NOT_FOR.md) — an app built on dark patterns isn't a showcase of this system, whatever template it started from.
4. Optional but wonderful: add a lesson you learned to [portfolio/RECENT_LEARNINGS.md](portfolio/RECENT_LEARNINGS.md) in the same PR.

## Questions, help, show-and-tell

Use the repo's **GitHub Discussions** tab (maintainers: enable it under Settings → Features → Discussions) — "Show and tell" for launches, "Q&A" for getting unstuck. Issues stay for bugs and concrete proposals; Discussions are for everything conversational.
