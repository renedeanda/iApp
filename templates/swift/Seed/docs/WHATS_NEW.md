# What's New — Seed

> Submitted to App Store Connect as the "What's New in This Version"
> field on every release. Keep it short, plain, and honest. Per
> docs/NOT_FOR.md §1, no exclamation-spam; per WHATS_ALLOWED.md, you
> can be warm.

## Version {{MARKETING_VERSION}}

(Edit before each release. Examples below.)

- A small visual refinement on the home screen.
- Faster sync when you have lots of items.
- Fix: the share sheet no longer dismisses early.

---

### Conventions

- Lead with the user-facing change, not the technical change.
- Avoid "improvements and bug fixes" — say what you changed.
- One line per item; ≤ 80 chars each.
- No emoji unless the app's identity is `playful-pastel` or
  `maximalist-collage` (then one emoji per line max).
- Localize to all tier-1 locales before submission.
