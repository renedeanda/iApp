# Official Apple sources

Open these before policy analysis because App Review rules are a living document.

- App Review Guidelines: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/
- Guideline updates/news: https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=a233fmpw
- Submitting for review: https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-submissions-to-app-review/submit-an-app-for-review/
- App privacy details: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/
- Privacy manifests: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/privacy_manifest_files
- Tracking transparency: https://developer.apple.com/documentation/apptrackingtransparency
- Offering account deletion: https://developer.apple.com/support/offering-account-deletion-in-your-app/
- Sign in with Apple / login-services guidance: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#sign-in-with-apple
- StoreKit and in-app purchase: https://developer.apple.com/in-app-purchase/
- Human Interface Guidelines: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/

## Freshness rules

1. Record the access date and the visible guideline revision date when available.
2. Cite the relevant numbered section, not a remembered paraphrase.
3. Use Apple documentation for blocker claims. Third-party sources may suggest questions but cannot establish policy.
4. State when a conclusion is an inference.
5. If Apple pages cannot be reached, label the policy portion `UNVERIFIED — OFFLINE` and continue with artifact/runtime facts.
6. Do not treat examples as exhaustive or convert words such as “may” into “will reject.”
