# ADR 003 — Monetization

- **Status:** Accepted
- **App:** Sprout
- **Authors:** worked example (Kindling)

## Decision

**Tier 0 — pure free, forever.** No IAP, no subscription, no tip jar at v1.

## Why

- Zero ongoing costs: on-device SwiftData, no AI, no hosted anything (ambition rung 1 per 016).
- The mission is a gift-shaped promise — "growing into proof" priced per-win would poison the log.
- Portfolio role (per the 5-tier ladder in portfolio/MONETIZATION_MATRIX.md): the acquisition-funnel / brand-handshake slot.

## Revisit trigger

If opt-in CloudKit sync plus a widget gallery ever grow into real scope, the honest move is Tier 1 lifetime IAP for the *extensions* — the core log stays free per the house covenant. Recorded so future-me doesn't relitigate from scratch.
