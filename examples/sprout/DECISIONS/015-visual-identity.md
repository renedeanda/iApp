# ADR 015 — Visual Identity

- **Status:** Accepted
- **App:** Sprout
- **Authors:** worked example (Kindling)

## Decision

**Warm-minimal** (cool-leaning variant via the Sage departure).

## Why

The mission is quiet accumulation; warm-minimal is the identity whose failure mode (blandness) is cheapest to escape through the one Breathing motion + the result-reveal delight. Brutalist would overpower five-second interactions; hand-drawn would promise whimsy the content doesn't sustain.

First app in this worked portfolio, so no diversity pressure — recorded for the next app's `/pick-visual-identity` run.
