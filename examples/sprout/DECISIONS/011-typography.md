# ADR 011 — Typography

- **Status:** Accepted
- **App:** Sprout
- **Authors:** worked example (Kindling)

## Decision

**Rounded-system** specimen (`Theme/Typography.swift`), weight-led hierarchy. Warm-minimal + a five-second gesture wants zero reading friction; rounded SF is the least assertive competent choice.

Serif and mono specimens remain swappable at `Theme/_TypographySpecimens/`.
