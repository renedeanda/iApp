# ADR 000 — Mission

- **Status:** Accepted
- **App:** Sprout
- **Authors:** worked example (Kindling)

## Decision

> *"Tiny daily wins, logged in five seconds, growing into proof."*

11 words. The subject is the user's wins, not the app. "Five seconds" is the constraint every screen answers to; "growing into proof" names the emotional payoff (evidence against the inner critic, not a streak to defend).

## Options considered

- *"A habit tracker that doesn't shame you"* — rejected: defined by what it isn't; leads with the competitor's failure.
- *"Celebrate small wins every day"* — rejected: an instruction to the user, not a promise from the app.

## Consequences

Forecloses: goals, plans, coaching content, social features. A "win" is logged after it happened — Sprout never tells you what to do. Everything in 013-anti-list.md derives from this.
