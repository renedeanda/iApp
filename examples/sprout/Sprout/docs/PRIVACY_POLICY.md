## TL;DR

- Sprout stores your data on your device and (optionally) in your private iCloud.
- Sprout does not track you, does not collect personal data, does not sell anything to anyone.
- Sprout has no servers. There is no account.

## What stays on your device

- Everything you create or configure in the app.
- Preferences, including any in-app purchase state (managed by StoreKit, not by us).
- Diagnostic information you choose to share via the system Settings → Privacy → Analytics.

## What goes to iCloud (only if you enable it)

- Your data, syncing through your **private** iCloud database.
- We never see your iCloud content. Apple's CloudKit terms apply.
- You can disable iCloud sync in Settings at any time.

## What goes to us

**Nothing personal.** No identifiers, no IP collection, no behavioral profiles.

If TelemetryDeck analytics are enabled (Settings → Privacy in-app),
the app sends anonymous aggregate signals (e.g., "session started")
that cannot be linked back to you. We use this only to understand
which features are useful enough to keep.

You can disable analytics in Settings, and the disabled state is
respected immediately on the next signal.

## What we don't do

- No advertising. No advertising IDs.
- No third-party data sharing.
- No SDKs that fingerprint or profile users.
- No "engagement" notifications. Notifications only happen when you
  schedule them yourself.

## Third-party services

| Service          | What it sees                              |
|------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| Apple StoreKit   | Purchase events. Apple's privacy terms.   |
| Apple CloudKit   | Your data in *your* private iCloud.       |
| TelemetryDeck    | Anonymous aggregate signals (opt-in).     |

## Children

Sprout is not directed at children. We don't collect personal data
from anyone, including children, so the COPPA / GDPR-K constraints
that protect minors don't have anything to constrain.
