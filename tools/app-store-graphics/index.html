Drop a screenshot into any slot (or leave it blank). Toggles restyle every card live; card 1 is your App Store hero. Per language: pick a Locale, then ↓ ZIP on a device for that locale — or ⬇︎ Everything to get every locale × device in one archive organized locale/size/…, ready to upload. Exports are exact App Store px at both Apple-approved sizes — iPhone 6.9″ + 6.5″, iPad 13″ + 12.9″ — so you can upload whichever your listing uses (each auto-scales to every smaller device). Use Chrome; PNG is crispest — switch to JPEG only if a reviewer ever flags an alpha channel.
